Stage 1: One-page Executive Briefing

Company in one paragraph

Observed Fact AUB Group (ASX: AUB) is an ASX200 group of insurance brokers and underwriting agencies. It takes equity stakes in owner-run broking and agency businesses and supplies group services (IT, back office, M&A, claims, risk and compliance). It earns mostly commission and fees on about A$11bn of premium placed for about 1.6m clients, across about 640 locations in 17 countries. Underlying revenue grew from A$541.6m (FY19) to A$1,596.6m (FY26), driven by the A$880m Tysers (Lloyd's wholesale) deal in 2022 and the A$432m Prestige (UK) deal in 2026. Observed Fact The direction now is integration and margin. One of six FY27 priorities is to "strengthen enterprise capability through technology, data and operational excellence", and the results deck says AI is moving from foundations to broker, underwriting and claims workflows.

Strong Inference The path runs from an SME broker network (Austbrokers, renamed AUB Group in 2015), to an acquisitive specialist group (BizCover, 360 Underwriting, Tysers, UK retail), to an integrated, data-led distributor with one data platform (Nexus), group AI governance and margin targets for every division. 2. How the Company Became What It Is Today

2005 to 2018: Broker network
Austbrokers bought stakes in Australian SME brokers and let the founders keep running them. It listed on the ASX in 2005 at about A$100m and renamed itself AUB Group in 2015 as it grew in NZ.
2019 to 2021: Build and buy
Mike Emmett became CEO in 2019. BizCover (2020) added a digital SME channel; 360 Underwriting (2020) and later SURA built an agencies arm; Health & Rehab was exited.
2022 to 2025: International
Tysers (A$880m) took AUB into Lloyd's wholesale and roughly doubled revenue. UK retail roll-ups (Momentum, Movo) followed.
Late 2025: Takeover cycle
EQT, then EQT and CVC, proposed A$45 a share and walked away after due diligence on 1 Dec 2025. Shares fell 17.8%.
2026: Integrate and compound
Prestige (A$432m) was funded by a discounted A$400m raise. NZ underperformed after an unsuccessful market share push. Inference AUB now presents itself as an integrator: one data platform (Nexus), group AI governance and margin targets for every division.

What is the Nexus Platform?

Observed Fact Nexus is AUB's internal group data platform. The FY26 results deck (25 Aug 2026, slide "AI Strategy: Foundations to Impact") describes it in one line: "Nexus, the AUB Data Platform, is building unified, AI-ready data across AU, UK and NZ." It sits under "Data foundation: scaling", beside Microsoft 365 Copilot (the enterprise AI interface) and the AI governance and delivery engines. "Building" means it is still in progress.

Not publicly disclosed

  • Technology stack (for example Snowflake, Databricks or Microsoft Fabric).
  • Start date, owner, budget, and which businesses are already connected.

Caution Not the same as Nexus Advisernet (Aust) Pty Ltd, an older broker-network entity in AUB's associates and subsidiaries list.

What it likely is

  • Strong Inference A central data layer joining data from AUB's acquired businesses (Austbrokers partners, Agencies, BizCover, Tysers, UK Retail, Prestige, NZ), which each run their own systems.
  • Strong Inference "AI-ready" points to a standard, governed data model that Copilot, the 43 AI agents and CoverNet can use safely: clients, policies, premiums, claims and commissions.

Why it matters for the role

  • Working Hypothesis Nexus readiness will set the pace for which AI use cases can reach production first (opportunity 2).
  • Closest match to your Cisco Snowflake and DBT work: warehouse volume down 42%, lineage and release controls, AI-readiness for 200+ internal customers (Stage 3, story 5).

Questions to ask

  1. What platform is Nexus built on, and how far through is it across AU, UK and NZ?
  2. Which data is already in Nexus, and which still sits in partner systems?
  3. Does this role work with the Nexus team on which AI use cases go first?

AI Delivery Lead

An enterprise organisation is looking for an AI Delivery Lead to help turn ideas into practical business outcomes.

Technical knowledge of AI, business acumen and the ability to deliver with a slim team will be required to be successful in this role.

Seeking someone who sits between business and technology, understands AI well enough to engage with technical teams, and can take ownership of opportunities from idea through to implementation, adoption and value realisation.

You'll be responsible for identifying and prioritising AI use cases, building stakeholder support, coordinating delivery, working with vendors and technology partners, and ensuring solutions are adopted and delivering measurable outcomes.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Identify and shape AI opportunities across multiple business functions.
  • Build business cases and define the value of AI initiatives.
  • Coordinate delivery across business stakeholders, technical teams, vendors and external partners.
  • Drive AI initiatives from concept through to implementation and adoption.
  • Assess solution options, challenge assumptions and work through delivery complexities.
  • Establish appropriate governance, risk and responsible AI controls.
  • Support AI capability uplift through training, enablement and change activities.
  • Track adoption, business outcomes and realised benefits.
  • Help build repeatable processes and ways of working that support the scale-up of AI across the organisation.

Desired experience

  • Experience delivering AI, automation, digital or data-driven initiatives.
  • Strong stakeholder engagement and influencing skills.
  • The ability to identify opportunities and turn ideas into executable plans.
  • Experience working across both business and technology teams.
  • Practical understanding of AI, GenAI, automation and modern technology platforms.
  • Experience navigating governance, risk, privacy and regulatory considerations.
  • Strong commercial thinking and a focus on measurable outcomes.
  • A proactive, self-starting mindset with a willingness to roll up your sleeves and get things done.

The ideal profile will have

  • Proven ability to take AI initiatives from discovery and business case development through to deployment, adoption and measurable business outcomes.
  • Strong understanding of AI governance, risk and compliance requirements, ensuring appropriate controls and guardrails are embedded throughout the delivery lifecycle.
  • Technically credible across AI, Agentic AI and LLM-based solutions, with the ability to engage with and challenge engineering, data and vendor teams when required.
  • Experience driving AI capability uplift, helping teams build the knowledge, skills and ways of working required to successfully deliver and scale AI initiatives.
